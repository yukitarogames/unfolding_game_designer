# Mechanics Dictionary

What's possible, and what makes it work. Terms are defined in [taxonomy.md](taxonomy.md).

## How to read this

- **Forms, not themes.** Each entry is a structural pattern that can be dressed in any theme.
- **Not a menu.** This dictionary doesn't prescribe which forms a game uses, where it uses them, or how to tune them.
- **Not complete.** Forms not listed here are welcome. Combining forms is welcome. A form being absent from this list is never a flaw in a design.
- **Read by the judge, not the designer.** The judge uses it to assess how well a design executes the forms it chose. The designer doesn't read it, so designs aren't drawn from a list.

## Entry format

- **What it is:** a neutral description.
- **Works well when:** opinion.
- **Fails when:** opinion.
- **With promises and unfolding:** opinion.

An opinion field marked _Open._ has no opinion recorded yet.

### Milestone tiers

- **What it is:** reset to tier up rather than to earn a currency. Each tier has harder requirements and gives specific rewards.

## Resource structures

### Single resource, vertical upgrades

- **What it is:** one resource; progress means producing more of it, faster.
- **Works well when:** _Open._
- **Fails when:** it's used where a mechanic needs depth. A better generator for one resource gives a jump in power and then very little.
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Several resource types with distinct jobs

- **What it is:** a mechanic produces several resource types, and each type is used to improve a different aspect of the mechanic.
- **Works well when:** there are many types and each one has its own job. Choices come from deciding which aspect to improve next.
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Producer tiers

- **What it is:** generators that produce other generators.
- **Works well when:** _Open._
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Conversion chains

- **What it is:** one resource is converted into another, possibly through several steps.
- **Works well when:** _Open._
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Caps and storage

- **What it is:** a limit on how much of a resource can be held.
- **Works well when:** _Open._
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

## Engines

Ways to progress within a mechanic. See [Engine](taxonomy.md#engine).

### Scaled purchases

- **What it is:** spend currency on repeatable upgrades whose cost grows.
- **Works well when:** _Open._
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Time-gated progress

- **What it is:** progress driven by timers rather than currency, e.g. research that completes over time.
- **Works well when:** _Open._
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Allocation

- **What it is:** a limited pool (resources, workers, capacity) assigned across several sinks, and reassignable.
- **Works well when:** the sinks lead toward different promises, so the player allocates toward whichever promise is most compelling to them. See [choices.md](choices.md).
- **Fails when:** _Open._
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Capacity expansion

- **What it is:** unlocking more space to work with, e.g. slots or plots.
- **Works well when:** it's slow, and there's more capacity to open than seems necessary.
- **Fails when:** most of the capacity opens early.
- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Stage progression

- **What it is:** advance through stages of increasing difficulty; clearing a stage opens the next.

- **With promises and unfolding:** _Open._

### Random draws

### Merging
