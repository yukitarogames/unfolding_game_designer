# Prestige

Principles for resets and prestige in an unfolding incremental game. Terms are defined in [taxonomy.md](taxonomy.md). For the different forms prestige can take, see [mechanics.md](mechanics.md#prestige-forms).

## It has to be worth it

What the player gets from a prestige must clearly be worth what they give up.

## It's optional

The player chooses whether and when to prestige.

---

## Other guidance

- **Usually a mid-development decision.** At some point, the developer decides the game needs a prestige layer, then plans where the player reaches it and what they get from it.
- **It doesn't have to be the whole game.** There are different ways to do it; you can prestige a specific mechanic.
