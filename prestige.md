# Prestige

Principles for resets and prestige in an unfolding incremental game. Terms are defined in [taxonomy.md](taxonomy.md).

- **Generally a good thing.** Most games will have prestige.
- **A mid-development decision.** Don't start development with prestige. At some point, the developer decides the game needs a prestige layer, then plans where the player reaches it and what they get from it.
- **Don't lean on it.** Introducing prestige early and leaning on it heavily makes you lazy about coming up with progress, mechanics, and unfolding.
- **It doesn't have to be the whole game.** There are different ways to do it; you can prestige a specific mechanic.

There's no single straightforward answer beyond this: **usually do it, but later on, and don't lean on it.**
