# Unfolding

Principles for how unfolds work and how mechanics connect. Terms are defined in [taxonomy.md](taxonomy.md). Examples are illustrative only, not things to copy.

---

## Connecting mechanics

### The point of a connection

A new mechanic's connection back to an existing one exists to **shorten the path to the next promise** in the existing mechanic.

Mechanic A became very slow: it was hard to make progress and the next promise was very far. The new mechanic shrinks that path dramatically.

> **Example (illustrative only):** the path to the next promise in mechanic A goes from a week to 5 hours.

**As long as this happens, the connection is doing its job.** Exactly how it happens is not that important.

### The bonus: change the shape of an engine

The best connections **change the shape of an [engine](taxonomy.md#engine)**: something rare, linear, and flat suddenly becomes something you can produce and scale.

> **Example (illustrative only):** a resource you only got a little of, on a timer, every few hours. Now you have a producer for it that you can upgrade. It goes from 10 a day to 50,000 a second.

It's nice to be clever here: to have the progress feel fresh, or to shorten the time to the next promise in a way that is interesting or surprising. **That's a bonus, not a requirement.**
